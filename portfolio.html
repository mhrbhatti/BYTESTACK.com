Featured Build

TestCraft + CodexCS

One Platform, Two Products

TestCraft is a MERN-based quiz and test creation platform built for educators. Teachers build structured question banks, schedule tests for their classes, and track student results in one dashboard — replacing scattered spreadsheets and paper-based grading with a single organized workflow.

CodexCS is a focused branch of TestCraft, built specifically for Computer Science exam preparation. Students practice chapter-based MCQ quizzes, earn XP as they progress, and get a full breakdown of correct, incorrect, and skipped answers after every attempt — turning practice into a feedback loop instead of a guessing game.

Both products share the same MERN backend and authentication system, letting us ship a focused student-facing tool without rebuilding infrastructure from scratch.
